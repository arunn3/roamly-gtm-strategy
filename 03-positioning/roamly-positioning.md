# Roamly Groups · Positioning Framework & Statement, Module 3

## Target Persona
- **Who they are:** The friend who always ends up planning: say a 20s-30s traveler who has booked on Roamly before and is now organizing a long weekend for seven friends. They hold the spreadsheet and send the Venmo requests. (Illustrative composite, not a real person.)
- **What they're trying to accomplish:** A great shared experience that actually happens, with everyone in and paid, without losing weeks to logistics or getting stuck with the bill.
- **What's getting in their way:** Chasing replies, fronting money, tracking who owes what, and the awkwardness of asking friends to pay. 45% of group travelers report financial conflict or discomfort.

## Positioning Framework
- **FOR** (Target Audience): The friend who ends up organizing everyone's group trip (5-12 people), already a Roamly user.
- **WHO WANT/NEED** (The Need): To get a whole group booked, paid and on the same plan without becoming the unpaid travel agent: no fronting the money, no chasing replies.
- **WE OFFER** (The Offering): Roamly Groups: group booking for curated local experiences, with booking, each person's payment and the shared plan in one flow.
- **BECAUSE OF** (Value Proposition): One link gets everyone in and paid: each person pays their own share, and the organizer never fronts the cost.
- **DESPITE** (Barriers): Roamly Groups is brand new with no group-specific track record yet; invitees must trust a platform they may not know and may need an account; V1 covers Roamly's own experiences (no flights or lodging) and is not built for corporate or very large groups.
- **BETTER THAN** (Differentiation): The DIY stack (group chat + Venmo/Splitwise + spreadsheet), which tracks money after the fact and leaves booking and fronting to the organizer, and tour marketplaces, which sell fixed group tours with no described split-payment flow. Differentiator: booking, payment and plan in one flow, on vetted local experiences (12,000+ hosts, 40 cities, 3x repeat booking).

## Positioning Statement
> For the friend who ends up organizing everyone's group trip who wants the whole group booked and paid without fronting the money or chasing anyone, Roamly Groups is the group booking tool for curated local experiences that gets everyone in and paid with a single link. Unlike a group chat, a payments app and a spreadsheet stitched together, Roamly Groups handles the booking, each person's payment and the shared plan in one place, with vetted local hosts.

## Pressure Test
- **Test run:** Competitor Discomfort, could a competitor use it word-for-word?
- **What it revealed:** Desk role-play (run by the drafter, not a human): read as a product lead at Viator / GetYourGuide or Airbnb, 'one link, everyone pays their own share' is the most copyable phrase, so on its own it is NOT ownable. What is hard to copy is the combination: a group flow plus vetted local hosts plus a repeat-booking record. The statement already carries 'curated' and 'vetted local hosts'; the fix is applied in messaging by leading with the organizer-relief outcome and Roamly's curation, not the payment mechanic alone. Other tests (desk review): Believable - conditional until beta data exists; Relevant - pass; Durable - pass, watch for positioning drift if corporate features are added.
- **Blind-read result:** NOT YET RUN with a human. To do: show the statement cold to one peer for 60 seconds with no explanation and ask who it is for and what value it delivers. Expected if it works: 'the person who organizes group trips' and 'they don't front money or chase people'. Failure signals: 'travelers in general' or 'a tour booking site'.

## Final Positioning Statement
> For the friend who ends up organizing everyone's group trip who wants the whole group booked and paid without fronting the money or chasing anyone, Roamly Groups is the group booking tool for curated local experiences that gets everyone in and paid with a single link. Unlike a group chat, a payments app and a spreadsheet stitched together, Roamly Groups handles the booking, each person's payment and the shared plan in one place, with vetted local hosts. (Unchanged after the test; the ownability gap is handled in messaging.) One-liner: Book the whole group in one link. Everyone pays their own share.
